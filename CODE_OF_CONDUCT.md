# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This community exists to help people with food allergies travel safely. We are committed to making participation a welcoming, respectful experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our standards

**Expected behaviour:**
- Be kind and respectful in all interactions
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people it serves
- Show empathy — many contributors are managing serious health conditions for themselves or their children

**Unacceptable behaviour:**
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Dismissing or minimising the severity of food allergies
- Sharing unverified or misleading safety information
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Safety-specific standards

Because this platform has real health implications, we hold contributors to a higher standard around data accuracy:

- Do not claim certainty you do not have about a food being safe
- Do not discourage others from verifying information independently
- Do not downplay the seriousness of allergic reactions

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub. All reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions or ban contributors who violate this code of conduct.
